**Prompt:**

Create a personal portfolio website for a backend-focused web developer, styled with the aesthetic of the *F1* movie (2025) — high-speed cinematic motorsport, sun-drenched pit lanes, carbon fiber and racing suits, the glamour and grit of Formula 1, and the precision of engineering under pressure.

**Color palette:** Deep racing black, carbon fiber charcoal, pit-lane concrete grey, and crisp paddock white. Bold accent colors drawn from F1 liveries — signal red (Ferrari), papaya orange (McLaren), racing green (Aston Martin), and electric blue. Highlights in checkered-flag white, warm asphalt amber, and the chrome glint of trophies. Sun-bleached desert tones for circuit backgrounds (Bahrain, Abu Dhabi).

**Typography:** A sharp, condensed sans-serif for headings — fast, technical, motorsport-grade (Monument Extended, Druk Wide, or Space Grotesk condensed). Clean geometric sans-serif (Inter) for body. A monospace (JetBrains Mono) styled like telemetry readouts — lap times, sector splits, tire temperatures. Numbers get special treatment — large, bold, often paired with units like "ms," "rpm," "°C."

**Layout & structure:**
- Hero section as a race-start grid — name set like a driver number on a car, tagline as a radio call, with a massive countdown or lap counter
- Chapter-based navigation styled like a pit wall dashboard: "DRIVER," "RACES," "SETUP," "RADIO"
- Project cards as race-weekend data cards — circuit name, lap time, tech stack as "car setup," outcome as "race result" (P1, P2, DNF)
- Section dividers as checkered flag strips, start-finish lines, or telemetry graphs
- A horizontal scroll option for the projects section — like scrolling through a season calendar
- Live-timing-inspired data layouts: tables, split times, delta indicators (+0.284s), position changes

**Visual details:**
- Textures: carbon fiber weave, brushed aluminum, asphalt grain, kerb stripes (red and white)
- Motion blur effects on hover — like a car passing at speed
- Speed-line overlays on hero and transition sections
- Telemetry-style data visualizations: fake throttle traces, G-force graphs, tire wear bars as decorative accents
- Checkered flag patterns as section accents or hover states
- Racing stripes down the sides of the layout
- A persistent "lap counter" or "session timer" ticking in the corner (purely decorative)
- Camera-flash effects on project card reveals — like a photographer catching the car at the apex
- Cursor trailed by a faint speed blur
- Optional ambient audio toggle: distant engine notes, pit-lane crowd, team radio chatter

**Content sections:**
- **Hero ("DRIVER #[number]"):** Name set like a driver's name on a car, tagline like "Precision at every lap" or "Backend engineering, race pace" — with a car number and team-style graphic
- **About ("THE DRIVER"):** Short, personal intro — written like a driver bio in a race program. Who you are, studies at CADT (your "academy"), work at BongKert Solutions (your "team"), framed as a career progression
- **Projects ("RACE RESULTS"):** Each backend project as a Grand Prix entry — circuit (project name), year, tech stack as "car setup," short race report with lap times and position gained. Include "podium" styling for standout projects
- **Stack ("CAR SETUP"):** Languages, frameworks, databases presented as car components — engine (language), chassis (framework), tires (database), aero (deployment) — each with a short technical note
- **Contact ("TEAM RADIO"):** Email, GitHub, LinkedIn styled like radio channels on a pit wall — "Box, box, box" humor optional, minimal and direct

**Tone:** Precise, confident, high-performance, a little cinematic. Treat backend craft like engineering a race car — every millisecond matters, every system tuned, built for pressure.

Build as a responsive single-page site with smooth scroll, speed-line transitions, motion-blur hover effects, telemetry-inspired data accents, carbon fiber textures, and an optional ambient engine audio toggle. Everything should feel fast, exact, and engineered — like a pit stop in web form.
